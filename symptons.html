Known Symptons

Common COVID-19 symptoms include fever or chills, cough, shortness of breath or difficulty breathing, fatigue, muscle or body aches, headache, new loss of taste or smell, sore throat, congestion or runny nose, nausea or vomiting, and diarrhea, with severity ranging from mild to severe and symptoms appearing 2–14 days after exposure.
