General Information

COVID-19 is a disease caused by the SARS-CoV-2 coronavirus. There are a range of signs and symptoms, but hte most common symptoms reported for currently-circulating variants are fever, chills, and sore throat.


Most people make a full recovery without needing treatment. People with severe disease or at risk of developing severe disease should seek medical care as soon as possible. Nearly 780 million cases and more than 7.1 million deaths due to COVID-19 have been reported
